About Food Waste

Food waste is a major global issue that affects the environment, economy, and food security. Millions of tons of food are wasted every year while many people suffer from hunger.

Causes of Food Waste

  • Overbuying and poor meal planning
  • Improper food storage
  • Large portion sizes
  • Lack of awareness

Prevention & Solutions

  • Plan meals before shopping
  • Store food properly
  • Donate excess food
  • Compost food waste

Food Donation

Excess food can be donated to NGOs, shelters, and food banks to help people in need and reduce waste.
